About Me

I am Pranay Narang, a 2nd year Computer Science and Engineering Student at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Pune. I chose the field of Data Science through Python and its Data Visualization tools it offers. In the year 2024, I started following Formula 1 (The pinnacle of Motorsports). Within a few months of watching races, I was more drawn towards the strategical decisions taken by the teams during races, the tyre degredation data analysis, timing charts and car performance. That is when I made it my goal to excel in the field of Data Sciences and pursue a career in Formula 1.
